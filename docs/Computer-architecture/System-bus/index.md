## wikipedia [System bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_bus)

A **system bus** is a single [computer bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bus_\(computing\) "Bus (computing)") that connects the major components of a computer system, combining the functions of a [data bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_bus "Memory bus") to carry information, an [address bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Address_bus "Address bus") to determine where it should be sent or read from, and a [control bus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_bus "Control bus") to determine its operation.
